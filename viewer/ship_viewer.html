Scene & appearance

Reading mesh…
Angle map: teal = grazing approach · red = near perpendicular. The camera sets the incoming direction. Back-facing normals show as unexposed; incorrect mesh winding requires inspection. This does not predict HP damage.

Mesh labels such as “Hitbox” and “AverageBox” are leads, not proof of server collision or damage multipliers. A texture is rendered only if the OBJ has UVs and a matching local game image is located. The XNB must be re-recovered with v3.9 or later so its material IDs are preserved.

Checking recovered geometry…

An open boundary edge is not by itself a missing face: real sails, gunports and deck openings may be open. Compare to the game client before classifying a hole.

Recovered client armor formula

Verified client IL: BasicArmor = PatArmor × (1 − AllStatFine) and Armor = BasicArmor × (1 + GetBonus(5)) + GetBonus(13). Enter raw fractional GetBonus values, not percentage-point numbers. Catalog Armor may differ from PatArmor.

Verified getter formulas: Common.dll methods 0x06001ee8 and 0x06001f6f. This is client-property evaluation, not verified damage or ricochet simulation. Bonus(18) bow reduction and Bonus(54) penetration are recovered accessors, but their downstream damage application remains unresolved.

Data provenance & limitations

“Relative shot index” = cannon poundage × ammunition damage factor, an illustrative comparison only. Geometric effective armor = catalog armor ÷ cos(impact obliquity). Neither is proven to be WoSB's server formula.

Port/starboard equality and the bow < side < stern damage ordering are user observations. No numeric regional multipliers are assigned without measurements. This tool does not inject packets or interact with the game client.
