Boarding simulator

Runs the game's boarding fight loop: crew is split into groups of 16 + places/15, each group strikes every 3 s (±5%) for its damage × (HP left)0.7, split across up to 3 target groups; musketeer defence cuts damage to ×0.2. Who wins when time runs out is decided by the server; the 1.5× strength rule used here is inferred.
