# WoSB Workbench 4.14.0

- **Ship builder** (Tools): pick a ship and Elite level, fill its upgrade slots, add special crew, a lantern and a figurehead. Speed, Defense, durability, maneuverability, hold and crew are worked out the game's way (base × percent bonuses + flat bonuses, with each upgrade's value for that ship rank); all other effects are listed in plain words. Share a build by link; every ship page links to it.
- **Personal Island planner** (Tools): building costs at your captain rank in the order you build (the game's formula), decorations with gold / Coins prices and bonuses, labour limit, labour per hour and warehouse size, for up to three islands, with the captain skills each extra island needs.
- **Chest simulator** (Tools): the Treasure Chest, Elite (Imperial) Chest, Inca Treasure Chest, New Year Chest and altar offerings, with the game's own reward weights, guaranteed-within counters, owned-ship skipping, the chance of every reward and what a number of opens costs in Coins.
- Ship pages link to the ship builder and to the crafting calculator ("Materials to build it").
