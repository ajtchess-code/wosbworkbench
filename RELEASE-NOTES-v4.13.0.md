# WoSB Workbench 4.13.0

- **Crafting calculator** (Tools → Crafting calculator): plan any ship, cannon, ammunition, consumable, ship upgrade (per ship rank) or material, several at once.
  - Follows every recipe down: workshop, furnace, Personal Island workshop, shipyard, cannon foundry and upgrade recipes from the game, with batch sizes, craft hours and gold.
  - Buy or craft per part (Auto picks the cheaper at your prices), "rawest materials" mode, a recipe choice where there are several, and a Personal Island option.
  - Your own prices, mixed price lots (`500@12, 2000@15`, cheapest first) and what you already have, used at every step.
  - Totals, spare amounts from batches, a step-by-step build order, copy buttons, "Save as image" recipe card, and share links kept while used and removed after 30 days unused (falls back to a self-contained link when the site database is not set up).
- Ship upgrade recipes are computed with the game's own formula for each ship rank.
- Privacy policy covers shared plans.
