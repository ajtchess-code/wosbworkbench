WoSB Workbench v4.3.4 — Compiled Effects Research + Damage Hypotheses
=====================================================================

This is the complete SOURCE + Windows builder, not a precompiled installer.

INSTALL / UPDATE
1) Extract this ZIP to a writable Windows folder.
2) Run Build-Update.cmd (Python 3.13 required for building only).
3) In your current Workbench, Updates -> choose:
   dist\WoSBWorkbench-4.3.4-Update.zip
4) Restart the Workbench when the offline updater completes.

SHADERS / SHIP RENDERING
1) In Discovery Center, choose Inspect compiled shaders.
2) Select C:\Games\World of Sea Battle\default\game (or your actual client).
3) Inspect the generated Shader-Audit.json and CompiledEffects folder.
4) For default ship and equipped designs, continue testing Ship 3D's
   Appearance-Diagnostics.json exports from v4.3.3.

DAMAGE HYPOTHESES
1) Mechanics Lab -> Combat damage hypotheses.
2) Select cannon/ammo, enter target armor, impact angle, and number of hits.
3) Compare hard-threshold vs smooth-transfer behavior. Their equations are
   HYPOTHESES, not WoSB's confirmed HP damage formula.
4) For real calibration, collect target pre/post HP for 3 or more controlled
   single-shot trials per ship, weapon, region, range and angle.

IMPORTANT
This version does not deliver original WoSB Direct3D/XNA shader behavior or
an exact damage prediction. Native shader decoding/parity needs real compiled
shader output and an inspection of client effect bindings. Packet timing is
not decoded hit damage, and named helper meshes are not proven hitboxes.
