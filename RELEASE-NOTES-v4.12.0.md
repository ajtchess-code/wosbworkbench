# WoSB Workbench 4.12.0

Wiki content batch 2.

- **Special crew** now use their in-game names (Lucky One, Corsair, Pilot…), are separated from boarding crew, grouped by type (Sailors, Combat, Pirates, Adventurers), and show per-sailor / per-boarder effects. New Special Crew overview.
- **Consumables** overview: every consumable with its cooldown, duration, recipe, gold cost and effects.
- **Captain ranks**: XP per rank, running total, the rank 30 cap and the fixed XP per level after it.
- **Elite status**: costs (Elite XP, gold or Coins) and benefits per level for ranks 1–5, on its own page and on each ship page.
- **Ship pages**: base build materials, shipyard time and the Battle Mark price to finish instantly.
- **Sails compared** and **Lanterns** pages.
- **Achievements**: bronze / silver / gold medal targets, rewards, ships they unlock; new Captain Titles and Medals page.
- **Cosmetics**: figurehead and lantern bonuses written out in full (multi-effect bonuses now read correctly).
- **Ammunition**: arc factor, minimum damage, shot radius, and effects in plain words.
- **Resources**: market minimum and maximum prices.
- Every effect code is now written in plain English across the site.
- Tables get per-column drop-down filters; custom sails without a picture are hidden.
