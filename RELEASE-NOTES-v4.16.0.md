# WoSB Workbench 4.16.0

- **Weapon 3D models look right again**: cannons, carronades, mortars and the rest are now shown at full detail with a sharp texture of their own. Before, the whole weapons sheet was shrunk to fit one size limit and its texture was cut to 512 pixels, so each gun got a blurry 64-pixel patch, and the viewer sometimes picked the low-detail copy. Lighting in the item viewer is also softer and closer to the game.
- **Personal Island in 3D**: the Island planner now shows your island with the buildings you plan on it. Pick which island to view; tick "Third Island look" (on by default with three islands) to see the bigger buildings the Third Island skill gives.
- **Decorations in 3D**: every decoration page has a 3D model, and the Island planner has a decoration preview. Multi-part models (a castle, a mill, a crane) are shown whole, with the ground patch they stand on.
- Island ground is drawn the game's way, from sand, grass and rock blended by height and slope, with the sea around it.
- The model pack format changed, so the wiki's model pack rebuilds once on the next wiki build.
