# WoSB Workbench 4.5.4

## Wiki: less filler, more data
- **Empty outlines removed**: about 180 placeholder research outlines ("Research status: not yet verified…") no
  longer appear on the public wiki.
- **Game guide**: the 31 Gamepedia entries now carry the game's own help text (they were title-only before), with
  links to the matching mechanics pages and tools.
- **New data overviews**, built from the game files with comparison bars:
  - **Ships by rank**: rank averages, then every ship in its rank side by side.
  - **Cannons compared**: per gun type, with penetration, damage per ball (and against armor 6), damage per minute,
    range, reload and firing arc.
  - **Upgrades by effect**: readable effects ("Reduce fire area damage +25%"), grouped by slot and by effect.
  - **Port battle rules**: tower and fort HP by shallows rank and team limit, the battle clock, respawn costs and
    capture rules.
- **Ship pages**: a "Compared with rank N" table (bars, rank average and place), handling (guns per side, wind rose,
  hardness) and one-click links into the battle simulator with that ship.
- **Category pages**: numeric columns show comparison bars, there's a filter box, and ships are ordered by rank.
- **Guides page**: reorganized into Game guide, Mechanics and Data overviews.
