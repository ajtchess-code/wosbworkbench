# WoSB Workbench 4.8.3: new main page and logo

- **New main page**, laid out like a classic game wiki front page:
  - a notice line with the data date;
  - a quick-navigation box;
  - a "Welcome to the WoSB Wiki!" panel with a banner and a "New visitors" guide;
  - a sideways-scrolling **What's new** row with this update and recently changed guides;
  - a **Sitemap** grid of boxes for Ships, Combat, World, Captain & crew, Economy, Cosmetics & islands, Guides and About
    the wiki, each with its own picture.
- **New logo:** the gothic "W" in the site's blues. It is used for the sidebar and mobile header logo, the favicons
  (`.ico`, SVG, 16/32 px), the Apple touch and Android app icons, the full logo with wordmark, and the 1200×630 social
  sharing card.
