# WoSB Workbench 4.15.0

New wiki pages, all from the game's own rules:
- **Events**: an overview plus one page each for Halloween, New Year, Anniversary and Spring Festival: start date, the 14 daily quests, rewards for days finished, the first-open gift, what changes at sea (golden fish, pumpkins, faster forts, snowballs…), the event ship, and the full event trader with prices.
- **Imperial forts**: the fort from above with all 27 guns and where they face, a ring showing how many guns reach each direction, the safest approach angles, the 15-minute loot crate and respawn notes.
- **Altars**: the full offering reward table with chances.
- **Wind, weather and currents**: one wind for the whole sea turning over 20 s, the four sea states with wave height and wind strength, storms and fog banks (how many, where they form, how fast they cross), shallow-water waves, and the whirlpool (the only current) moving every 12 hours.
- **Store**: the kinds of sale and their discounts, Captain's Seal prices, services, chest prices and every design sold with its Coin price (skins for rank 5–7 ships 34% less). Design tables now show the store price.
- **NPC ships**: Empire and event ships plus the ships NPCs sail, with pictures and links to their 3D models.
- **Ship upgrades** table shows each effect for a rank 7 ship and a rank 1 ship, and Budget/Standard.
- "Monets" now reads "Coins" everywhere; achievement pictures confirmed.
